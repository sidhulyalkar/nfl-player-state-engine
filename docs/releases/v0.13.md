# v0.13.0 — Drive Volume & State Laboratory

v0.13.0 adds an isolated drive-volume challenger and the historical replay needed to decide whether pace and possession-start realism deserve a place in the research simulator.

## Added

- `DriveVolumeModel` with recency-weighted hierarchical pace context;
- empirical offense/opponent/league drive-start field-position mixture;
- scrimmage-play drive extraction and observed drive-volume diagnostics;
- `simulate_matchup_volume_probe`, a research-only simulator with component-specific RNG streams;
- explicit simulated drive count, plays/drive, seconds/play, and start-field-position draws;
- eight-cell expanding weekly replay across play-calling, opportunity allocation, and drive-volume switches;
- within-team-season pace-label permutation negative control;
- v0.13 drive-volume research promotion gate;
- v0.14 evidence router;
- staged smoke/full GitHub Actions workflow;
- benchmark API/version updates to v0.13.0;
- regression tests for drive extraction, point-in-time fitting, deterministic simulation, drive metrics, and fail-closed promotion.

## Why this release is separate from v0.12

The v0.12 simulator samples `seconds_between_plays` from the same empirical row that supplies yards, first downs, turnovers, and touchdowns. Possession resets also use fixed field positions.

v0.13 does not silently replace those mechanics. It builds a parallel probe where pace and drive starts can be switched independently and measured against the established baseline.

## Primary experiment

The central comparison is:

```text
learned_state_drive
        vs
learned_state_legacy
```

Both cells use the learned play-call model and state-conditioned player allocator. Only drive-volume mechanics differ.

## Negative control

Pace labels are permuted within team-season while preserving each team's marginal pace distribution. Real state-conditioned pace must beat both team-only pace and this permuted control before the gate can clear.

## Promotion boundary

A cleared v0.13 gate means only:

> the drive-volume challenger is eligible to become the research generative champion after manual review.

It does not automatically:

- change `/v1/research/game-intelligence/simulate`;
- change direct player projections;
- change draft rankings;
- change fantasy production decisions;
- assert improved real-world predictive accuracy.
