## Correctness finding: pace-label alignment

The historical `seconds_between_plays` field is attached to the current play but represents game-clock elapsed **since the previous play**. That is useful as a retrospective timing feature, but it is not the correct label for predicting how much clock should run after the current simulated play.

v0.13 therefore adds:

```text
seconds_to_next_play = current play clock - next same-drive offensive play clock
```

The forward label is computed only within the same game, possession/drive, and offense. When an upstream drive id is missing, a possession-change sequence is used rather than allowing the same team's separate possessions to bleed together.

The new pace model trains and scores on `seconds_to_next_play`. The established v0.12 outcome sampler is intentionally left unchanged in the legacy replay cells so the historical benchmark measures the new mechanism rather than rewriting its baseline.

Because the forward label is only observed when a drive continues, learned in-drive pace is applied only to simulated continuing-drive states. Touchdowns, turnovers, punts, field goals, and failed fourth downs remain on the common legacy transition timing until a dedicated possession-transition model is tested.

## Why this release is separate from v0.12

The v0.12 simulator samples `seconds_between_plays` from the same empirical row that supplies yards, first downs, turnovers, and touchdowns. Possession resets also use fixed field positions.

v0.13 does not silently replace those mechanics. It builds a parallel probe where forward-aligned in-drive pace and drive starts can be switched independently and measured against the established mechanism baseline under standardized component RNG streams.

The legacy cells are therefore **mechanism-equivalent baselines inside the v0.13 probe**, not byte-identical reproductions of old v0.12 random trajectories.

## Negative control

Forward-aligned pace labels are permuted within team-season while preserving each team's marginal pace distribution. Real state-conditioned pace must beat both team-only pace and this permuted control before the gate can clear.

## Promotion boundary

A cleared v0.13 gate means only:

> the drive-volume challenger is eligible to become the research generative champion after manual review.

It does not automatically:

- change `/v1/research/game-intelligence/simulate`;
- change direct player projections;
- change draft rankings;
- change fantasy production decisions;
- assert improved real-world predictive accuracy.
