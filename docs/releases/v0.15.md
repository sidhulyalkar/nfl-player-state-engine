# v0.15.0 — Fourth-Down Decision & Drive-Termination Laboratory

v0.15 extends Fourth Down Lab with an explicit research layer for how offenses choose to end fourth-down possessions and for whether a scrimmage play terminates its drive.

## What changed

### Fourth-down policy

- canonical raw-PBP `GO / PUNT / FIELD_GOAL` extraction
- recency-weighted hierarchical `FourthDownDecisionModel`
- analytic frozen v0.14 heuristic probabilities
- team/context shrinkage
- season/field-zone/distance-preserving negative control
- isolated multiclass log-loss, Brier and action-accuracy diagnostics

### Drive termination

- scrimmage-level continuation / termination labels
- `DriveTerminationHazardModel`
- team/context shrinkage
- down/field-zone-preserving permutation control
- held-out binary log-loss and Brier diagnostics
- explicit diagnostic-only authority boundary

### Simulation

- new parallel `simulate_matchup_decision_probe`
- seventh RNG stream reserved for fourth-down policy
- first six v0.14 streams preserved
- frozen heuristic RNG consumption retained even when the challenger makes the actual decision
- direct fourth-down decision and go-attempt counts in team draws
- termination hazard scored without mutating possession outcomes

### Replay

- four-cell transition × decision attribution
- expanding weekly cutoffs
- fourth-down frequency metrics alongside existing drive, special-team, scoring, opportunity and fantasy metrics
- strict `v015_decision_promotion_gate`
- evidence-driven `recommend_v016_development`

### Operations

- package/API version 0.15.0
- v0.15 research benchmark artifact root
- manual smoke/full historical benchmark workflow
- benchmark CLI runner and report
- CI coverage for the new runner and tests

## Evidence boundary

This release implements research infrastructure. It does not claim a real out-of-sample predictive gain.

The live `/v1/research/game-intelligence/simulate` endpoint continues to use the established simulator path. Neither the v0.15 decision policy nor the termination hazard is automatically promoted.

The action policy may become eligible for manual research-champion review only after the expanding historical replay passes its preregistered isolated and downstream gates.

The termination hazard cannot become generative merely by fitting well. A separate terminal-family model is required before it can terminate drives in simulation.

## Recommended validation sequence

1. run the v0.15 smoke workflow;
2. run held-out 2023–2025 replay with 2021–2022 warm-up history;
3. inspect isolated action and termination negative controls;
4. inspect paired transition-on decision-off vs transition-on decision-on metrics;
5. segment residuals by team, distance, field zone and season;
6. use the v0.16 router to choose the next layer.
