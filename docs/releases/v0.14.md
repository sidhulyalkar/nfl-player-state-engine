# v0.14.0 — Possession Transition & Special Teams Laboratory

## Summary

v0.14 adds a research-only possession-transition layer around the game simulator. It is the next experimental layer after v0.13 separated continuing-drive pace from generic play outcomes.

The release does **not** promote a new production model. It creates the evidence, simulator probe, frozen replay, negative controls, and promotion gates required to determine whether terminal possession mechanics deserve authority.

## New research surfaces

### Raw-PBP transition evidence

A new transition extractor reads raw play-by-play instead of the scrimmage-only intelligence frame. That distinction is necessary because punts and field-goal attempts are often separate non-scrimmage rows.

The extractor maps a terminal possession event to the first scrimmage play of the next offensive possession and records:

- transition family
- previous offense
- next offense
- source field position
- next starting field position
- transition clock runoff when meaningfully observable

Terminal possession families are touchdown, turnover, punt, made field goal, missed field goal, turnover on downs, halftime, and fallback/other.

### Hierarchical possession-transition model

`PossessionTransitionModel` estimates next-possession field position and transition timing from recency-weighted historical evidence. Contextual estimates shrink toward transition-family priors when evidence is sparse.

The same layer contains a deliberately simple field-goal calibrator that begins with distance-bucket make rates and permits team evidence to move the probability only as sample weight accumulates.

### Negative controls

Transition start-field and timing targets are permuted within transition-type × season groups. Field-goal outcomes are permuted within distance-bucket × season groups.

The controls preserve broad historical marginals while destroying the contextual relationship the challenger claims to exploit.

### Parallel transition simulator

`simulate_matchup_transition_probe()` is separate from the established simulator path.

It adds:

- learned terminal transition timing
- learned next-possession starts
- empirical field-goal calibration
- direct Monte Carlo counts for punts, field-goal attempts/makes, turnovers, and turnovers on downs

The first five stochastic component streams are aligned to the v0.13 simulator seed construction. A sixth transition-only stream is added. When the new model replaces a v0.13 drive-start draw, the corresponding v0.13 tempo draw is still consumed and discarded so later continuing-drive pace draws remain aligned.

The opening possession is **not** treated as a halftime transition.

### Expanding frozen replay

The v0.14 benchmark fixes learned play calling and state-conditioned opportunity allocation, then crosses drive-volume and transition switches:

- `legacy_drive_legacy_transition`
- `drive_legacy_transition`
- `legacy_drive_transition`
- `drive_transition`

The primary transition comparison is `drive_transition` versus `drive_legacy_transition`.

Each test week is evaluated with models trained only on earlier chronology.

### Isolated and downstream evaluation

Isolated evaluation measures:

- next-possession start-field MAE
- transition-time MAE
- field-goal log loss and Brier score
- transition-type/distance baselines
- permutation-control performance

Full simulation additionally evaluates:

- plays
- pass rate
- points
- drives
- plays per drive
- continuing-drive pace
- starting field position
- punts
- field-goal attempts and makes
- turnovers
- turnovers on downs
- player carries/targets
- fantasy distribution loss when archived outcomes are available

Realized special-teams counts are computed directly from raw PBP so an end-of-game punt or field goal cannot disappear merely because no later offensive possession exists.

## Research promotion gate

The gate fails closed and requires broad historical evidence. Default requirements include three held-out seasons, at least 200 games, sufficient transition and field-goal event counts, wins against isolated baselines and permutations, full-simulation starting-field improvement, weekly consistency, and no material downstream regression.

A cleared gate means only **eligible for manual research-champion review**.

It never changes production projections automatically.

## Historical workflow

`.github/workflows/v014_transition_benchmark.yml` supports:

- smoke mode for plumbing validation
- full expanding replay over the requested historical window
- immutable benchmark artifacts
- Markdown job summary

Default full evidence window:

- source history: 2021–2025
- held-out seasons: 2023–2025
- regular-season weeks: 1–18

## Claim boundary

Passing CI, synthetic tests, or the smoke workflow does not establish predictive improvement. The scientific claim requires the full point-in-time historical replay.

## Next release

v0.15 is deliberately not preselected. The benchmark routes the next experiment toward latent drive strategy, transition-action generation, kicker/weather calibration, decomposed scoring transitions, drive termination/duration, or additional evidence depending on which residual remains after v0.14 replay.
