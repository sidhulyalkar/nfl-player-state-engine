# v0.16.0 — Terminal-Family Generation Laboratory

v0.16 closes the explicit modeling gap left by v0.15: knowing that a possession is likely to end is not enough to generate a coherent football world. This release adds a research-only terminal-family layer, historical outcome conditioning, and an eight-cell frozen replay designed to determine whether that layer deserves any simulator authority.

## New research mechanism

The canonical target is:

`CONTINUE / SCORE / TURNOVER / DOWNS / END_HALF`

A new `TerminalFamilyModel` separates:

1. probability the current offensive scrimmage play ends the possession;
2. terminal family conditional on an ending.

The second stage cannot hide behind the overwhelmingly common `CONTINUE` class.

## Corrected possession semantics

v0.15's binary hazard was intentionally diagnostic and defined around the final offensive scrimmage play of a drive. That is not sufficiently precise for generation.

v0.16 explicitly treats a failed third down followed by a punt or field goal as `CONTINUE`. The fourth-down policy still owns the next action. Only a direct score, turnover, failed fourth-down attempt, or real half/game expiry is given terminal-family meaning.

## Coherent outcome generation

The release extends `EmpiricalPlayOutcomeModel` without changing its established `sample()` path.

When terminal authority is enabled in the v0.16 probe:

- terminal family is sampled first;
- a historically observed outcome row compatible with that family is sampled next;
- yards, completion, turnover, touchdown, interception, fumble and first-down fields therefore remain coupled to a real play outcome;
- sparse incompatible cells fail back to the exact frozen outcome and emit a measurable fallback rate.

No classifier label is converted directly into invented player statistics.

## RNG attribution hardening

The v0.15 legacy outcome draw is still consumed on every play before a terminal intervention. Terminal family and terminal-conditioned outcome use deterministic shadow streams derived from the pre-draw state and do not advance the established outcome RNG.

This keeps future legacy draws aligned across A/B cells instead of allowing stochastic drift to masquerade as lift.

## END_HALF authority

`END_HALF` is structurally restricted to a short true clock-boundary window. It changes clock runoff, not football scoring. The inherited halftime state machine remains responsible for the next possession.

## Eight-cell frozen replay

The new benchmark crosses:

- legacy / learned possession transitions;
- legacy / learned fourth-down decisions;
- legacy / learned terminal families.

The resulting eight variants measure terminal-family lift under four different parent world-model contexts. Learned play calling, state-conditioned opportunity and v0.13 drive-volume logic remain fixed.

## Isolated evaluation

The terminal model is scored with:

- multiclass log loss;
- multiclass Brier score;
- top-label expected calibration error;
- per-family Brier and recall;
- canonical termination Brier/log loss;
- conditional terminal-family log loss and accuracy.

Negative controls include:

- full terminal labels permuted within broad season/down/field-zone context;
- terminal type permuted while exact termination labels remain fixed.

## Promotion gate

`v016_terminal_promotion_gate` fails closed unless there is enough multi-season evidence and the model:

- beats the observable-state context baseline;
- beats full and conditional permutation controls;
- remains calibrated;
- has negligible terminal-conditioned outcome fallback;
- improves direct non-clock terminal event frequency across most parent contexts;
- stays safe on drive volume, pace, scoring, opportunity, fantasy, special teams and fourth-down decisions.

A pass grants only eligibility for manual **research champion** review.
