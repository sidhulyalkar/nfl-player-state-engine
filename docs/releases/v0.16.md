# v0.16.0 - Terminal-Family Generation Laboratory

v0.16 closes the explicit modeling gap left by v0.15: knowing that a possession is likely to end is not enough to generate a coherent football world. This release adds a research-only terminal-family layer, historically coherent outcome conditioning, and an eight-cell frozen replay designed to determine whether that layer deserves any simulator authority.

## New research mechanism

The canonical target is:

`CONTINUE / SCORE / TURNOVER / DOWNS / END_HALF`

`TerminalFamilyModel` separates:

1. probability the current offensive scrimmage play ends the possession;
2. terminal family conditional on an ending.

The second stage therefore cannot hide behind the overwhelmingly common `CONTINUE` class.

## Simulator-matched possession semantics

v0.15's binary hazard was intentionally diagnostic. v0.16 needs a stricter generative ontology.

A failed third down followed by a punt or field goal remains `CONTINUE`; fourth-down policy still owns the next action.

Historical labels now mirror the frozen simulator exactly:

1. touchdown / goal-line crossing -> `SCORE`;
2. otherwise lost possession -> `TURNOVER`;
3. otherwise failed fourth-down conversion -> `DOWNS`;
4. otherwise true half/game expiry -> `END_HALF`;
5. otherwise `CONTINUE`.

First-down realization uses either the explicit first-down flag or yards gained reaching distance to go.

## Coherent outcome generation

The release extends `EmpiricalPlayOutcomeModel` without changing its established `sample()` path.

When terminal authority is enabled in the v0.16 probe:

- terminal family is sampled first;
- a historically observed outcome row compatible with that family is sampled next;
- yards, completion, turnover, touchdown, interception, fumble and first-down fields remain coupled to an observed football outcome;
- sparse incompatible cells fall back to the exact frozen outcome and emit measurable fallback evidence.

No classifier label is converted directly into invented player statistics.

## Structural legality hardening

Authority support is constrained before sampling:

- `DOWNS` is impossible before fourth down;
- `END_HALF` is impossible outside the short structural clock-boundary window.

If a sparse learned cell places all probability on illegal families, normalization falls back only across legal support. A generic uniform fallback cannot reintroduce an impossible terminal event.

## RNG attribution hardening

The v0.15 legacy outcome draw is still consumed on every play before a terminal intervention. Terminal family and terminal-conditioned outcome use deterministic shadow streams derived from the pre-draw state and do not advance the established outcome RNG.

This keeps future legacy draws aligned across A/B cells instead of allowing stochastic drift to masquerade as lift.

## Requested vs realized accounting

The simulator now distinguishes the terminal family the challenger requested from the family actually realized by the executed outcome row.

When conditioning falls back, the bridge records the family of the frozen legacy outcome and removes stale `END_HALF` clock authority when necessary.

Full-simulation terminal metrics are reconstructed from realized team draws rather than requested-family counters. Requested-vs-realized mismatch remains diagnostic evidence and is a promotion blocker.

## END_HALF authority

`END_HALF` is structurally restricted to a short true clock-boundary window. It changes clock runoff, not football scoring. The inherited halftime state machine remains responsible for the next possession.

## Eight-cell frozen replay

The benchmark crosses:

- legacy / learned possession transitions;
- legacy / learned fourth-down decisions;
- legacy / learned terminal families.

The eight resulting variants measure terminal-family lift under four different parent world-model contexts. Learned play calling, state-conditioned opportunity, and v0.13 drive-volume logic remain fixed.

## Isolated evaluation

The terminal model is scored with:

- multiclass log loss;
- multiclass Brier score;
- top-label expected calibration error;
- per-family Brier and recall;
- canonical termination Brier/log loss;
- conditional terminal-family log loss and accuracy.

Negative controls include:

- full terminal labels permuted within broad season/down/field-zone context;
- terminal type permuted while exact termination labels remain fixed.

## Promotion gate

`v016_terminal_promotion_gate` fails closed unless there is enough multi-season evidence and the model:

- beats the observable-state context baseline;
- beats full and conditional permutation controls;
- remains calibrated;
- has negligible terminal-conditioned outcome fallback;
- has zero requested-vs-realized semantic mismatch under the tested contract;
- improves realized non-clock terminal event frequency across most parent contexts;
- stays safe on drive volume, pace, scoring, opportunity, fantasy, special teams, and fourth-down decisions.

A pass grants only eligibility for manual **research champion** review.
